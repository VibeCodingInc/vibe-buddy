# THE MELD — first interface for a temporary shared cortex

Design branch only · founder-local · no production UI · `?meld=<state>`
Screens: docs/meld/{before,shapeshift,collision,third,return,absence}.png

**The law this interface obeys:**
> The system renders the COLLISION. It never authors the CONCLUSION.
> The third thought appears in the human, not on the screen.

The permanent interface stays conversation + composer. Every state below is
a TEMPORARY REVEAL over that surface, dismissible, one at a time, or absent.

## THREE AUDACIOUS CONCEPTS

### 1 · THE PRISM — your question comes back unrecognisable
Not options, not a summary: the SAME line re-formed as each lens touches it.
"should the notification layer have one owner or stay sovereign?" turns
through time → discipline → forgotten self → and returns as
**"who is responsible for a message that no one has answered yet?"**
The gesture: hold the thought and it turns. You never receive an answer —
you receive your own question, changed. (SHAPESHIFT is the animation of
this: earlier forms fade but stay visible, so you can see the rotation.)

### 2 · THE COLLISION FRAME — two worlds, one frame, no verdict
Both edges' facets side by side, each labeled with the WORLD it came from
(not just the person). Then one line naming THE GAP — the structural
difference between the questions each side was actually answering. Then it
stops. The empty space below the gap is the interface. That space is where
the third thought forms, and nothing may be printed into it.

### 3 · RETURN CHANGED — the conversation ends by altering its own cause
When a thought changes, the surface offers exactly one thing: the work it
came from. Affected artifact, the change it implies, apply/edit/don't.
The magic is directional: insight leaves the conversation and lands in the
project, issue or decision that produced the tension. A conversation that
cannot change anything is not thinking, it is talking.

## THE FOUNDER-LOCAL PROTOTYPE — built on a REAL collision
Six states, real context: the Aug-23 SSL-pin decision, which was a genuine
bilateral collision between two edges with different worlds.

- **your Mind** (this machine, this hour): "pin the ISRG root, not the leaf —
  a root outlives routine rotations, so this exact breakage cannot recur."
- **the platform coordinator** (fleet history; who is awake at the next CA
  rotation): "do NOT re-pin. Remove custom pinning. Any future pinning
  policy needs its own review, backup pins, and a rotation plan."
- **the gap**: one edge asked WHICH PIN. the other asked WHETHER PINNING IS
  A THING THIS TEAM CAN OPERATE.
- **the third thought** (neither side arrived with it): *the pin was never
  the decision — the decision was whether we can carry a rotating
  obligation at all. we can't, so we don't take one.*
- **returned**: Build 67 shipped with pinning removed and the policy gate
  written into the code comment. Verified on device.

That collision already happened, in this repo, this week. The prototype
renders it rather than inventing one.

## 60-SECOND WALKTHROUGH — before → collision → third → return
- **0:00 BEFORE** — a thread and a composer. One message: the dead pins.
  Nothing else on screen. No card, no badge, no panel. *(before.png)*
- **0:10 THE TURN** — the thought rotates through lenses and returns
  unrecognisable. You have not been answered; you have been re-asked.
  *(shapeshift.png)*
- **0:25 THE COLLISION** — two facets, two worlds, one frame, and THE GAP.
  The system stops mid-air. *(collision.png)*
- **0:40 THE THIRD THOUGHT** — a human types the line neither side arrived
  with. It appears in the composer, because a person wrote it. *(third.png)*
- **0:55 RETURN** — "this changed something · return it?" → Build 67 →
  apply. The conversation alters the work that caused it. *(return.png)*

## THE EXACT MOMENT THE RECIPIENT'S EDGE ENTERS
Not at send. Not at read. **At the moment they answer a question they could
not have answered from the message alone.**

Concretely: their edge enters when their reply contains a SOURCED FACET —
a claim carrying its own evidence from their world. Until then, a reply is
just a reply, and the interface shows nothing. The instant a sourced facet
arrives, the collision frame becomes available to BOTH sides — and only
then. This is already live: the Uriel message asks him what he lost while
his boundary was in the wrong place; if his answer carries a source, his
edge has entered and the Meld begins. If not, it was a good conversation.

Consent shape: each side approves only their own facet. Neither side ever
sees the other's corpus, index, or reasoning — only the one facet that
crossed, with its source, date and authorship.

## THE WILDCARD — THE ABSENCE *(absence.png)*
A verified negative, rendered as an experience:
> **you have never once written about timing as a selection filter.**
> two independent sweeps · ~40 vocabularies · vault, sessions, agents,
> research, essays. nothing on opening a door too early. nothing on waiting
> as a way of choosing. on this, you are reasoning from scratch.

This is real — it came out of MM#6. It is the only interface element that
gets MORE valuable the LESS it finds, and no messaging product has it: proof
of the shape of your own ignorance, sourced. It cannot be faked, it cannot
be a suggestion feed, and it tells you when to stop trusting your instinct
because your instinct has never been here.

## SMALLEST PRODUCTION SHAPE — ~80% of the magic
**One line above the composer, one temporary reveal, one return prompt.**

1. **The line** (offer or silence, no chrome): retrieval fact with true
   authorship — "SOLIENNE's practice recorded this (Apr 2026)… ›"
2. **The reveal** (tap, temporary, dismissible): the collision frame — both
   facets, both worlds, the gap. Never a tab, card, badge or panel; it
   closes and the conversation is exactly as it was.
3. **The return prompt** (only when a thought demonstrably changed): the
   affected work + apply/edit/don't.

What that keeps: the collision, the gap, the human authoring the third
thought, and the return. What it defers: prism animation, threshold, the
absence display, past-selves ceremony.

What it must NOT become, at any size: a tab, a memory dashboard, an insights
feed, a suggestion stream, or an assistant persona. The Mind stays invisible.
If the surface can't hold it in one line and one reveal, it isn't ready.
